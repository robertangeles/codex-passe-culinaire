# Product copy evidence

Checked 25 September 2026 at https://www.culinaire.kitchen/ using the gstack browser. This verifies the product website's public description, not an independent functional test.

The product describes preparing a prioritised task list from the day's menu and identifying components shared between dishes. It also describes inventory, purchasing, recipe costing and waste records.

Approved copy approach: use a restrained prep-list example beside any mention of AI. Do not repeat timing, accuracy, savings, trial terms or testimonial claims without separate confirmation. Keep the first visit focused on understanding the company and exploring the product.

Possible plain sentence: “CulinAIre Kitchen uses AI to help organise a prep list from today's menu, including components used in more than one dish.”

Recheck factual copy before launch because the product can change.

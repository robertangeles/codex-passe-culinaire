## About introduction

The 4 October 2026 founder direction calls for bridging kitchen craft and technology, bringing fragmented operational work together, and becoming a technology partner to chefs and restaurant owners. The introduction frames this as an ambition rather than a completed product claim. Industry context: [Toast Australia on disconnected restaurant systems](https://pos.toasttab.com/au/blog/on-the-line/when-restaurant-systems-dont-talk-to-each-other) describes the fragmentation problem, while [Square for Restaurants](https://squareup.com/us/en/restaurants/capabilities?section=keep-business-flowing) shows that integrated platforms already exist. Avoid absolute claims that the culinary world has seen no innovation or that Passe Culinaire is first.

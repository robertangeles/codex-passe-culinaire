Follow the working day

Every action leaves
a useful record.

  1. 01

    Start with the forecast

    Review expected covers, the menu and recipe requirements before deciding how much each section should prepare.

    Forecast & preparation
  2. 02

    Draw stock for mise en place

    Draw the ingredients needed for mise en place from the pantry. Keep the source and destination clear.

    Inventory & stock transfers
  3. 03

    Prepare the ingredients

    Prepare ingredients against the recipe: peel and portion the pears, prepare the filling and assemble the batch.

    Recipes & mise en place
  4. 04

    Bake, cook and get ready

    Begin baking or cooking and keep the section’s preparation plan up to date as food becomes ready for service.

    Preparation & service readiness
  5. 05

    Record usage and wastage

    Complete the stock record after preparation. In this illustrative batch, 3.0 kg drawn equals 2.4 kg used, 0.3 kg returned and 0.3 kg of peel waste.

    Stock usage & waste
  6. 06

    Inspect and receive deliveries

    Inspect deliveries as they arrive, check quantities against the order and record the accepted stock. Follow up shortages with the supplier.

    Purchasing & receiving
  7. 07

    Keep the team in step

    Manage the roster through the day and share preparation updates and handovers so the team can work from the same plan.

    Rostering & messaging
  8. 08

    Upload the day’s sales

    Upload the day’s sales data. Compare actual covers and dishes sold with what was forecast and prepared, then review the differences.

    Sales upload & review
  9. 09

    See the whole working day

    Bring the day’s records together to inform the next preparation plan. The illustrative pear balance is 8.0 kg opening stock, less 3.0 kg drawn, plus 0.3 kg returned and 10.0 kg received: 15.3 kg remaining.
